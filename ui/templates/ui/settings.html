How to retrieve your cookies from your browser

1. Open leetcode.com and sign in to your account.

2. Open Developer Tools (F12 or Ctrl+Shift+I / Cmd+Option+I).

3. Navigate to Application (Chrome) or Storage (Firefox) → Cookies → https://leetcode.com.

4. Copy the values for LEETCODE_SESSION and csrftoken and paste them above.
